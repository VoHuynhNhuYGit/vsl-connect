# Dấu ấn Việt Nam artwork

`frontend/src/assets/heritage-landscapes.webp` is original decorative artwork generated with the built-in image generation tool, inspired by the user's photographs of golden rice terraces, Hạ Long Bay and Hồ Gươm. It is an artistic montage of separate places, not one literal geographic panorama or a documentary photograph. The optimized WebP is 1200 × 800 with alpha transparency.

`HeritageArtwork.tsx` shares this scene between the homepage, theme selector and feature-page banners. Independent SVG clouds, water lines, reflected light and decorative leaves animate gently. All effects respect the background-motion setting, admin reduced-motion setting and `prefers-reduced-motion`. Theme-selector previews stay still. No database changes or new dependencies.

## Generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Create a stunning original Vietnamese landscape travel illustration for a website theme, inspired by three destinations: golden northern rice terraces at sunrise, Ha Long Bay limestone islands and a wooden boat, and Hanoi Hoan Kiem Lake with its small historic Turtle Tower and willow branches. Design an elegant cohesive decorative travel collage with three clearly legible vignettes in a landscape 3:2 composition: terraced rice hills and distant blue mountains in upper left, tall lush limestone karsts rising from turquoise water and a tiny Vietnamese wooden tourist boat in upper right, calm green lake with a small grey three-level Turtle Tower on a grassy islet in lower foreground, its reflection and graceful willow leaves framing one side. Soft glowing sunrise between mountains, rich golden rice, deep green trees, cobalt and turquoise water, warm apricot light. Detailed sophisticated hand-painted watercolor and gouache illustration, delicate textured landscape contours, luminous scenic beauty, crisp enough at 500px wide, airy margins. This is an artistic montage of separate Vietnamese places, not one geographically literal panorama. Entire composition contained within the canvas with softly feathered painterly edges and genuine alpha transparency outside. No large opaque rectangular background, no words, lettering, map, border, tourist badges, unrelated monuments, human close-ups, photographic collage, 3D or neon. Keep the iconic low Turtle Tower small and tasteful, no tall pagoda spire.
