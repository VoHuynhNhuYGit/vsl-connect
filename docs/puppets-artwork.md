It is an artistic illustration, not a photograph or a sign-language demonstration. The optimized WebP is 1200 × 800 with alpha transparency.

`PuppetsArtwork.tsx` shares the scene between the homepage, theme selector and feature-page banners. Independent SVG water ripples, reflected gold lines and warm stage lighting animate gently. All effects respect the existing background-motion preference, admin reduced-motion preference and `prefers-reduced-motion`. Theme-selector previews stay still. No new dependencies or database changes.
